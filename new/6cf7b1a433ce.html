The 2026 Land Rover Defender 110 P500 X-Dynamic SE is the heavy-hitter of the Defender lineup. This Canadian-spec beast swaps the mild-hybrid engine for a 493 hp 5.0L supercharged V8, cutting the 0–100 km/h time down to a blistering 5.4 seconds. It is a vehicle that commands respect, whether it's the guttural roar of the quad-exhaust or the aggressive X-Dynamic styling featuring Gloss Black wheels and blacked-out accents.

Inside, the 2026 model year is defined by the massive 13.1-inch Pivi Pro curved glass touchscreen, providing a high-definition hub for everything from off-road telemetry to the Meridian™ Surround Sound System. Despite its performance credentials, it remains a true Defender at heart, boasting a 3,720 kg towing capacity and the legendary Terrain Response® 2 system. For the Canadian driver, the P500 X-Dynamic SE offers the perfect blend of supercharged muscle and rugged, go-anywhere utility.
