The 2026 Land Rover Defender 110 P400 X is the pinnacle of the inline-six lineup, designed for those who demand the highest level of off-road prowess combined with bespoke luxury. Powered by a 3.0-liter I6 Mild-Hybrid engine producing 395 horsepower and 406 lb-ft of torque, the Defender X reaches 100 km/h in a swift 6.1 seconds. It comes standard with Land Rover’s most advanced hardware, including the Electronic Active Differential and Terrain Response 2 with a dedicated Dynamic Program. With a massive towing capacity of 8,201 lbs and the ability to wade through nearly a meter of water, the Defender X is as much a precision tool as it is a statement of intent.

Inside, the Defender X transforms into a luxury sanctuary packed with state-of-the-art technology. The cabin is anchored by a 13.1-inch Pivi Pro touchscreen and a high-definition Interactive Driver Display, complemented by a standard Head-Up Display that projects navigation and off-road data directly into your line of sight. Audiophiles will appreciate the 700W Meridian™ Surround Sound system, while the ClearSight interior rear-view mirror ensures perfect visibility regardless of cargo.

The standard sliding panoramic roof floods the cabin with light, while HD Matrix LED headlights provide superior illumination for night-time adventures. Safety is uncompromising, featuring a 3D Surround Camera system, Wade Sensing, and a full suite of driver assistance tools like Adaptive Cruise Control with Steering Assist. To ensure long-term performance, every 2026 model includes Land Rover’s comprehensive maintenance support, giving you total peace of mind as you explore the great outdoors.
