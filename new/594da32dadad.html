The 2026 Land Rover Range Rover Sport P460e Dynamic SE is a tour de force of sustainable performance, successfully bridging the gap between silent electric commuting and long-range grand touring. Combining a refined inline-six engine with a potent 160 kW electric motor, it delivers a massive 454 hp and an impressive 85 km of pure electric range—more than enough for the average Canadian daily commute without burning a drop of gas. The 2026 model stands out with its ability to utilize DC Rapid Charging, allowing you to top up the battery to 80% in under an hour. Inside, the "Dynamic SE" trim provides a sanctuary of Windsor leather and high-tech interfaces, centered around the massive 13.1-inch curved glass Pivi Pro screen. With standard Dynamic Air Suspension providing a glass-smooth ride and Land Rover’s legendary off-road systems like Wade Sensing, the P460e is the ultimate "no-compromise" luxury SUV for the eco-conscious driver in Canada.
