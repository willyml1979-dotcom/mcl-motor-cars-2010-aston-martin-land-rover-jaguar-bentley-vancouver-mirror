The 2026 Land Rover Range Rover P530 SE (Long Wheelbase) 7-Seat represents the absolute pinnacle of full-size luxury versatility, extending the vehicle's footprint to accommodate three rows of world-class seating without losing its majestic, minimalist silhouette. Characterized by a completely flat waistrail finish, flush deployable door handles, and hidden-until-lit vertical taillights, it commands an unmatched road presence. For the discerning Canadian buyer, it is the ultimate expression of elite executive transport combined with uncompromising family utility.

Beneath the sculpted hood lies a ferocious 4.4L twin-turbocharged V8 engine with a 48V mild-hybrid system, generating a massive 523 hp and 553 lb-ft of torque. Routed through an 8-speed automatic transmission and intelligent all-wheel drive, this sprawling luxury fortress leaps from 0–100 km/h in an astonishing 4.8 seconds. To effortlessly handle its extended length, standard All-Wheel Steering drastically reduces the turning circle for tight urban parkades, while predictive electronic air suspension continuously reads Canadian roads ahead to deliver an unshakeable, cloud-like ride.

Inside, the SE LWB trim transforms the cabin into an ultra-premium digital oasis designed for seven adults. The dashboard is anchored by a floating 13.1-inch curved Pivi Pro touchscreen featuring haptic feedback and seamless wireless smartphone integration, operating in tandem with next-generation Active Noise Cancellation built directly into the headrests to block out highway drone. Wrapped in sumptuous Windsor leather, it pampers everyone with multi-zone climate control, a massive panoramic glass roof, a crystal-clear Meridian™ Sound System, and power-folding seats that open up to reveal a cavernous cargo arena.
