This exceptional 2026 Land Rover Range Rover Sport Dynamic SE is a crown jewel in our luxury performance inventory, seamlessly combining track-inspired dynamics with executive-tier opulence. Under the sculpted hood rests a ferocious 4.4-litre twin-turbocharged V8 engine—the highly coveted "P530" powertrain—unleashing a massive 523 horsepower and 553 lb-ft of torque. Managed by a lightning-fast 8-speed automatic transmission and intelligent all-wheel drive, this absolute powerhouse launches from 0 to 100 km/h in a breathtaking 4.5 seconds, while standard dynamic air suspension delivers absolute composure through changing Canadian winter conditions.

The exterior profile of this performance SUV is aggressively elevated by premium factory enhancements that command an undeniable presence. The Black Exterior Pack swathes the grille, hood vents, and tailpiece lettering in high-gloss black trim, creating a flawless, high-end stealth appearance. This sinister aesthetic pairs perfectly with jaw-dropping, upgraded 23-inch wheels in Gloss Black (Style 5135) that fill the arches beautifully, framed by contrasting Red Brake Calipers. Ready for weekend adventures, the factory Towing Pack integrates advanced trailering technologies and a robust receiver hitch to unlock the vehicle's massive towing capacity with total peace of mind.

Inside the bespoke cockpit, progressive luxury meets cutting-edge innovation. The cabin is wrapped in a stunning dual-tone Caraway and Ebony Perforated Windsor Leather seat trim, featuring premium 20-way electric heated and ventilated front seats to ensure absolute comfort in any climate. The tech-forward environment is completely optimized by the Technology Pack, which adds a Head-Up Display and the ClearSight interior rear-view mirror, alongside the Convenience Pack for hands-free cargo access and power-folding utility. Centered around a beautiful 13.1-inch curved Pivi Pro touchscreen, the sensory experience is capped off by the immersive Meridian 3D Surround Sound System, delivering an unforgettable, concert-like drive every time you hit the road.
