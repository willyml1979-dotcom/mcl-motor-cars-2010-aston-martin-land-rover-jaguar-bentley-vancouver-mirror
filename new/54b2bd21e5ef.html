Embrace the perfect fusion of rugged capability and modern refinement with this exceptional 2026 Land Rover Defender 110 P300 S. Engineered to masterfully handle both demanding Canadian winter trails and urban streets, this iconic four-door adventurer is powered by a responsive 2.0-litre turbocharged inline-four engine producing 296 horsepower and 295 lb-ft of torque, seamlessly balanced by a robust 8-speed automatic transmission, intelligent permanent All-Wheel Drive, and Terrain Response®. The exterior commands an intimidating road presence courtesy of the stealthy Black Exterior Pack, which forms a striking contrast against massive 22" Wheels - Gloss Black (Style 5098). Step inside the highly advanced cabin to discover the upscale touchpoints of the Premium Upgrade Interior Pack, draped in ultra-premium Ebony, Windsor Leather Seats, and anchored by Land Rover's massive 13.1-inch Pivi Pro touchscreen infotainment system and a 3D Surround Camera. Fully optimized for cold climates and first-class passenger comfort, this highly optioned model features the Cold Climate Package (including a heated windshield, heated steering wheel, and heated washer jets), 40:20:40 Folding Heated Rear Seats w/ Centre Armrest, and the coveted Air Suspension Pack for adjustable ground clearance and a cloud-like ride.
