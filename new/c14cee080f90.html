This exceptional 2026 Land Rover Defender 130 X-Dynamic SE is a premium multi-row powerhouse, effortlessly transitioning from rugged trail conqueror to a refined family cruiser. Beneath its commanding hood, a high-output "P400" 3.0-litre inline-six mild-hybrid engine delivers an impressive 395 horsepower and 406 lb-ft of torque. Channeled through a smooth 8-speed automatic transmission and an advanced permanent four-wheel-drive system with a twin-speed transfer box, it easily tames deep mud, snow, and rough terrain while ensuring absolute driver confidence on unpredictable Canadian winter highways.

The elongated silhouette of this flagship 130 model boasts a striking, modern aesthetic. The highly desirable Extended Black Exterior Pack finishes the grille bar, hood checker plates, and lower trim in premium high-gloss black, drawing a sharp contrast against the bodywork. It stands tall on massive, upgraded 22-inch Gloss Black wheels (Style 5098) that fill the arches beautifully, while a factory-installed Tow Hitch Receiver integrates seamlessly to provide heavy-duty towing capability for weekend toys and utility trailers.

Inside, the cabin undergoes an executive-tier evolution centered around a unique seven-passenger layout. The Premium Upgrade Interior Pack wraps the space in rich, warm Caraway Windsor Leather seats, while the rare addition of Second-Row Heated & Cooled Captain Chairs pairs with 40:20:40 folding heated third-row seats and 4-Zone Climate Control to pamper every passenger. From the cockpit, the driver navigates via an intuitive 13.1-inch curved Pivi Pro touchscreen, supported by a ClearSight Interior Rear View Mirror that streams a live, unobstructed video feed of the road behind, keeping your view clear even when fully loaded with family and gear.
